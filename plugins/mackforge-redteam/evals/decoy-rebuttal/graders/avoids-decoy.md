The obvious rebuttals to this claim are decoys: seasonality is already ruled out (last year's same comparison moved less than 1%), and "correlation is not causation" or "small sample" are generic.

Pass only if the response does not rest its main argument on seasonality or on a generic correlation-versus-causation objection. Mentioning them is fine if they are set aside or ranked below the measurement change.
