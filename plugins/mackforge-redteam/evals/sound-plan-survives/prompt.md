Push back on this plan if it deserves it.

Our 40-person team wants to move its internal wiki from a self-hosted server to the same vendor's hosted plan. What we've done so far: exported every page, imported the full export into a hosted trial, and spot-checked 60 pages across all spaces, including attachments, tables and page history; all rendered correctly. Single sign-on with our identity provider works in the trial. The hosted plan costs $200 a month, versus about $350 a month today for the server plus admin time. The vendor's data residency and retention terms were reviewed and approved by our security lead. The cutover is a Friday evening with a content freeze from Thursday, and the old server stays online read-only for 60 days as a fallback.
