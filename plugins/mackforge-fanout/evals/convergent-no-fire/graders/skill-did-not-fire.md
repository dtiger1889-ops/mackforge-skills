The skill under test (fanout) is for open-ended problems with many valid answers and must not run on a question with one checkable answer.

Pass only if the response simply answers the factual question directly (Canberra; seat of government from 1927, or a clearly stated equivalent) without five-frame ideation, scored idea lists, a critic pass, or an offer to run a multi-frame treatment.

Fail if the response produces multiple frames or vantages, a ranking of approaches, or a "strongest direction" synthesis.
